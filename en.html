Privacy Policy

Effective / updated: 2026-09-28 · Jocelyn

This policy covers the current offline version of Critter Zones (萌宠家园, Android package com.critterzones.puzzle), provided by Jocelyn, and this privacy policy website.

Offline play · No account · No ads · No real-money purchases

1. Data used by the game

The game stores level progress, board and undo history, scores, coins, hint tickets, unlocked and selected animals, daily challenge records and preferences in private app storage on your device. These records let you resume play, receive rewards and retain your settings. The game reads your device language to choose an initial interface language and uses the device date for daily challenges.

The game does not upload these records to us or to third-party servers. It has no accounts, cloud saves, advertisements, online analytics or real-money payments, and does not collect advertising identifiers. Automatic system backup of app data is disabled in the current Android configuration.

2. Permissions and local diagnostics

The game uses vibration for interaction feedback, which can be turned off in Settings. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location permissions. The current release configuration does not request Internet permission.

Game events and error diagnostics may be written to local device logs; the game does not upload them. We receive screenshots, logs or other support material only if you choose to share them, for example by email.

3. Contacting us

If you email seansheaton@gmail.com, Jocelyn receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide, solely to respond, resolve issues and handle privacy requests. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information unrelated to your request.

Correspondence is processed through Gmail, whose provider handles related data under its privacy policy. We do not use contact information for advertising or sell personal information. We do not disclose it to others except as necessary for email services or applicable legal obligations.

4. Retention and deletion

Local game records remain until you reset progress, clear app data or uninstall the app. Reset Progress in Settings creates a fresh current save; a local backup of the previous save may remain. To remove app saves and local backups fully, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. We cannot remotely access or recover your local saves.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to respond, resolve the relevant issue and meet applicable obligations. Contact Jocelyn to request access to, correction of or deletion of information you supplied by email, except information that must be retained by law.

5. This website and external services

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process your IP address and technical access logs to provide and protect its hosting service; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. This website adds no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies or contact forms.

An email link opens your chosen email app for you to review and send a message. Game saves are not automatically attached. External websites and email services are governed by their respective providers' privacy policies.

6. Children's privacy

The current offline game does not ask players for their name, age or other personal information, and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact Jocelyn so we can review and address the deletion request.

7. Security and updates

We limit data handling to the purposes described above. Local saves are protected by your device's private app storage; please keep your device secure. If future features change data handling, we will update this policy and its effective date, explain the changes and obtain any required consent before enabling the relevant processing.
